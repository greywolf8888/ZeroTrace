## Decisions produced by research

1. Reuse protocol decoders and read APIs behind adapters; do not duplicate mature parsing.
2. Keep the canonical data/evidence model independent of any provider or platform schema.
3. Keep AGPL/GPL systems outside the Apache core unless an explicit review approves another model.
4. Treat missing/unclear licensing as a hard reuse boundary.
5. Public endpoints are convenient smoke-test inputs, not production evidence infrastructure.
6. Discover deployments and mechanism versions at the target snapshot; do not hard-code “current”
   behavior into historical analysis.
7. GMGN remains an optional observation source, not a launchpad classifier or identity oracle.
